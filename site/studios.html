The Problem

Why Most Branded Video Fails.

It is not a production problem. It is a thinking problem. Brands brief a camera crew, show up on shoot day, and hope the result looks good. It usually does. But looking good and performing well are two different things.

Video that performs starts with a clear strategic brief. It is scripted for a specific audience with a specific goal. The format, the length, the platform, and the call to action are all decided before a camera is switched on. That is how we work.

Made to Be Watched

Our Approach

Think First. Film Second.

01

Brief

We start with your business goal, not a camera spec. What do you need this content to achieve? Who is watching it? Where will it live? What should they do after they watch it?

02

Strategy

We define the content format, the platform strategy, the tone, and the message hierarchy. Every decision at this stage saves time and money at every stage after it.

03

Script

We write the script. Every word is intentional. Whether it is a thirty-second brand story or a sixty-second product explainer, the script is the most important deliverable in the process. We do not skip it.

04

Production

We handle the shoot. We direct the talent, whether that is your own people, professional actors we source, or an AI-generated format. We manage the location, the crew, and the session so you do not have to.

05

Edit and Deliver

We edit to brief, not to taste. You receive platform-optimised video files, correctly formatted for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or wherever the content is going. Ready to post.

Format Menu

Eight Formats.
Every Business Need.

Tell us what you need to communicate. We will tell you which format fits best.

001

ExecutiveReel

Short form video positioning founders, CEOs, and senior leaders as visible authorities in their industry. Scripted, coached, and produced for LinkedIn and Instagram. The highest-return content format for executives who want to build a public profile.

Best for: Executives and founders building their personal brand.

002

BrandStory

A thirty to sixty second video that communicates who your company is, what you stand for, and why you exist. Emotional, shareable, and built for top of funnel brand awareness. The first video most brands should make.

Best for: New brands launching. Established brands refreshing their positioning.

003

ExplainerClip

Clear, concise video explaining what your product or service does and why it matters. High conversion value for landing pages, sales decks, and social media. Especially effective for complex or unfamiliar offerings.

Best for: Tech companies, financial services, any brand with a product that needs explaining.

004

ProofPlay

Customer or staff testimonial videos, lightly scripted and professionally produced. One of the most effective trust-building formats available. Consistently underused by Nigerian brands despite being consistently the most believed.

Best for: Any business that wants social proof in a format people actually watch and trust.

005

TeachShort

Educational content that teaches something genuinely useful in under sixty seconds. Builds loyal audiences faster than promotional content because it gives value before it asks for anything. The format that turns followers into clients.

Best for: Professional service firms, financial brands, healthcare, executives building thought leadership.

006

AdCreative

Short form video built from the brief stage for paid campaigns on Meta, Google, and TikTok. Performance-focused from the first word of the script. Delivered in multiple versions for A/B testing. Designed to convert, not just entertain.

Best for: Any business running paid social or digital advertising campaigns.

007

ActorLed

Fully produced branded content using professional actors sourced and directed by Fineries Studios. For brands that want a polished, narrative-driven format without using internal staff. We handle casting, direction, and delivery.

Best for: Consumer brands, product launches, campaigns requiring specific talent or character types.

008

AIClip

AI-generated video for brands that need high volume, fast turnaround, or multiple format variations without a physical shoot. The right tool for ad creative testing, explainer content, and digital campaigns where speed and scale matter most.

Best for: Brands needing volume, speed, and multiple variations for testing and digital campaigns.
